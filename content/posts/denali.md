Denali is my rescued husky, born December 14, 2015, and she's the one who taught me that force free training has its limits. I used e-collars and light aversive training with her for a couple of years before she became the well-behaved dog she is now.

These days I can trust her with no collar. I still put the e-collar on around prey animals and traffic. I can call her off a rabbit with a verbal cue, but it's not worth the risk to me.

I don't need her to do complex tasks or be a show-off like Griz, so for the most part her training is done. We mostly train now because she enjoys it, and she crashes a lot of Griz's sessions. Here she is on a full lure for a sendaway, a drill she didn't know. She's learned from me before and understands luring pretty well, so she makes it look a little easier than it is with a green dog.
