You can feed your dog kibble. A lot of people do, and their dogs live long, full lives. I had one amazing dog who couldn't digest kibble, and that sent us down a long road of raw food.

We've done it a lot of ways. At times we saved money by getting livers, hearts, kidneys and the rest from farmers. That was gross for me and gross for the dogs. Yeah, dogs don't like the way organs taste. I've checked with about 5 dogs, and 5 out of 5 didn't like them. They'd eat it if they were hungry enough, but they'd pass if they weren't starving.

Then I moved to premade raw grinds for a while, and that was OK. But if you watch documentaries, even wolves eat berries, grass, and other non-meat stuff, and kibble already has fruit, veggies, and supplements. So we went looking for non-meat ways to get the micronutrients. You can make your own and save money, but I lack time, so I use Dr. Harvey's.

The instructions say to mix it with hot water, meat, and oil. If I do that, Denali won't eat it. So at dinner, when Denali's eating, I mix it with an egg instead of hot water. In the morning it's just hot water and some meat for Griz. She'll eat anything.

They also get tons of treats, and I usually share what I'm eating: blueberries, carrots, and yogurt through the week. Griz gets most of the table scraps to beef up on calories. If we have something super healthy like salmon and broccoli, both dogs get some, and I cut some of Denali's food ahead of time.
