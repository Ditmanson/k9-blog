An e-collar is an invisible leash. E-collars have always been amazing to me. I like them for negative reinforcement, and I keep the stimulation low.

I use Educator collars, which put both of my dogs on a single remote.

{{< youtube Ki8EG31XYqU >}}

## Like a prong, from farther away

I use an e-collar the same way I use a [prong collar](/posts/prong-collar/). There's one big advantage and one drawback.

| | Prong | E-collar |
| -- | -- | -- |
| It is | A leash | An invisible leash |
| Distance | As long as the leash | Way out there |
| Direction | Yes | No |

A leash can show your dog which way to go. An e-collar can't. So I layer it with leash pressure training to fill in the direction.

## Leash pressure comes first

That's the rule: the dog should understand leash pressure before we start on the e-collar.

1. **Teach [leash pressure](/posts/escape-and-avoidance/).** They learn to move with pressure.
2. **Layer on the e-collar.** It's the same lesson, with a new kind of pressure.
3. **Take it farther.** Now the leash can be invisible.

## Let them get used to it

I promote collar neutralization. That's a period of time where dogs wear the collar without it being used, so they have time to get used to it. Typically they're a bit irritating and itchy at first.

## Start low

I typically work at 15% power and go up if the dog doesn't respond.

{{< level 15 >}}

Where you end up depends on:

- **The dog.** They're all different.
- **The environment.** How much stimulation is it competing with?
- **Desensitization.** How much has the dog been through?

## It doesn't have to be a secret

There's more than one way to do this. Some trainers keep the collar a secret, so the dog never learns where the stim comes from. I prefer my dogs knowing it comes from the collar.

| | Kept secret | What I prefer |
| -- | -- | -- |
| Also called | Collar-dumb | Collar-savvy |
| The dog knows it's the collar | No | Yes |
| Can you switch later? | Yes | No, they can't unlearn it |

When they know, they have a say. They can move with the pressure and turn it off, or avoid it completely.
