I used to think of e-collars as punishment, best used when the dog treats it like a superstitious superpower. Then I worked both styles. Negative reinforcement is easier and it works better, and an e-collar is the best tool for it there is.

Here's where the levels usually land on my dogs:

| Situation | Level |
| -- | -- |
| No distractions | 10-15 |
| Mildly distracting environment | 20-25 |
| Punishment | 30-40 |

The collar goes to 100. I've never seen a reason to go past 40.
