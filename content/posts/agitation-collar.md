An agitation collar is built to be pulled on, hard. It looks like a big flat collar, but it has a wider surface area with padding that collapses inward, so when the dog yanks against it, it gives a little. It's safe: the padding keeps her from getting choked and hurting her throat.

## What it's for

They're called agitation collars because they're used to create frustration. We want the dog agitated and frustrated, pulling against it, and that brings her drive up for any task.

Frustration builds drive, and I like training with heavy, heavy drive.

You see them a lot in protection clubs. I don't train protection, but I use it the same way.

## The upside

It's the best collar for building drive. There's no collar better for building play drive on a dog who's already peaking with conventional [play](/posts/food-and-play/).

They're also a ton of fun to work with, and they make your dog look cool.

## The mistake

People use them like they're normal collars. These things are built to be pulled on, hard. For walking and [leash pressure](/posts/escape-and-avoidance/), use a [prong collar](/posts/prong-collar/).

## How I use it

1. Tie her up, or have someone hold the leash.
2. Get her excited with a treat or a toy, just out of reach.
3. She pulls into the collar. As she gets crazy, mark "yes."
4. She gets to play with it.
