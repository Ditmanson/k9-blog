An agitation collar is the one that's built for the dog to pull against. It looks like a big flat collar, but it spreads pressure across a much wider area and it's padded for the dog to push into, so the risk to the throat is close to zero.

You see them a lot in protection clubs, where they're used to bring a dog's drive up. I use it the same way, and not just for protection. It builds drive for any task. For tracking, I swap between this and a [harness](https://k9-communication.com/faq/harness/). They're interchangeable.
