Griz's primary collar, the one with her tags on it, is a slip collar my wife made. I use it all the time, and it doubles as an 18" slip lead.

Slip collars are great for continuous pressure, which makes them good for negative reinforcement ([escape and avoidance](https://k9-communication.com/faq/escape-and-avoidance/)). You don't get the snappy corrections of a pinch collar, but it sure looks nicer.
