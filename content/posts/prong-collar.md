A prong collar, or pinch collar, looks mean. It's also less likely to injure your dog than a [flat collar](https://k9-communication.com/faq/flat-collar/), because the pressure is spread around the neck instead of landing on the throat. I use a Herm Sprenger.

What I get from it is clear, adjustable pressure, momentary or constant. Corrections can start very light and escalate only as needed. It gets a bad rap because it can be used for positive punishment. That's sometimes necessary, but it's not the collar's main job.

## Get the size right

Better too tight than too loose. The collar should rotate on the dog, but make full contact all the way around the neck when the martingale is pulled tight. Too loose and a correction hits a smaller surface area, which can injure your dog. Two dogs in loose prongs can also get their collars tangled in a fight.
