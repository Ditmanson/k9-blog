Dogs need an appropriate outlet for their energy and instincts. That's what enrichment is: a chance to run, sniff, explore, play, and use their brains.

Sometimes that's just a walk. This is a long one in the woods with both my dogs off leash. They wear e-collars and I have the remote ready, but I never use it. No real training here. The dogs get to run, sniff, and explore. This is the payoff.

## When a walk isn't enough

- **Nose games.** Hide a ball or some food and let your dog find it.
- **Tracking.** Any dog can learn it, starting with food in the footsteps. It's a lot of mental work. Finish with fetch and you've exercised body and mind together.
- **Food and play training.** It's enrichment, exercise, and obedience in one.
