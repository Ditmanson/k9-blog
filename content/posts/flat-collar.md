Flat collars are the most common collar out there, and the least useful for training. They look nice, but a correction puts all the pressure on the throat.

I do still use one on occasion, mostly for tracking. I run the long lead between her legs, so the pressure lands on the back of the neck instead of the throat. I never make aversive corrections on the track. But if she pulls into the lead this way, it turns her head in the direction of the scent.
