## Search and rescue

Griz is a search and rescue dog, working toward certification with both NASAR and SARDOC (Search and Rescue Dogs of Colorado).

Here she is trailing my son Cassius, showing she can find her kid if he gets lost in the woods. To her it's a game, and the reward at the end is one of her favorite people. To me it's practice for the day one of my kids really does wander off.

## Why not protection?

We started it, and I dropped it for two reasons:

- **It takes a team.** I didn't realize how much work we'd need from outside people.
- **It might conflict with search and rescue.** At least that's how I see it, and search and rescue is her job.

We kept the muzzle training, and we do it through play. She puts the muzzle on and we wrestle.

## The hard part

Griz is afraid of off-leash dogs. She was bitten twice as a puppy during her fear periods, and that's on me. I had oversights on the environment. I learned from it, and I've been rehabilitating her since.

I manage it with obedience, enforced with one or both of these:

- **[Leash pressure](/posts/escape-and-avoidance/)**
- **The promise of her favorite toy, the ball on a rope**

When she's focused on me for a heel, she worries less about the rest of the world.
