Griz is my high-drive working line German Shepherd, born October 21, 2025. She's a search and rescue dog with Search and Rescue Dogs of Colorado, and we train for IGP: tracking, obedience, and protection. Everything I teach, I train on her first.

## Trailing games

I walk two red balls out into a field, one past a road crossing. Red is hard for a dog to see, so Griz has to use her nose. Then I spin her around and cast her to each ball, one at a time. It's a game, and it's also impulse control, direction at a distance, and nosework all at once.

## Tracking at 12 months

This is competition-style tracking with a dog who's still too young to compete. She follows the track footstep by footstep with her nose down. It's a lot of mental work.
