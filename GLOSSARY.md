# K9-Communication LLC's Blog

The casual dog blog at griz.sh, companion to the k9-communication.com business site. Replaces the old griz.sh site, starting fresh with no carried-over posts or comments.

## Language

**Post**:
One dated blog entry, belonging to exactly one Category and any number of Tags.
_Avoid_: Article, entry, Dog record (that's k9-communication.com's term)

**Category**:
The single top-level bucket a Post lives in: Gear, Dogs, Adventures, Training, Food, or Other. Changing it never changes the Post's URL.
_Avoid_: Section, topic

**Tag**:
A free-form label on a Post; a Post can have many.
_Avoid_: Keyword, label

**Card**:
A Post's preview in the Feed: thumbnail, title, date, Category, excerpt, Tags.
_Avoid_: Tile, teaser

**Feed**:
The scrolling grid of Cards, newest first, filterable by Category or Tag.
_Avoid_: List, index, timeline

**Comment**:
A reader's message on a Post.
